SIX-FIGURE LOGIC

YOUR RATING 1000 ± 350
THE GOAL
  • Assign values to each of the six letters A through F so as to satisfy all clues.
THE RULES
  • Each letter A–F must be assigned a different integer from 1 to 10.
  • Every letter must be assigned — even those that don't appear in any clue.
  • The solution must satisfy all clues simultaneously and the rules stated above.
  • Each puzzle has exactly one correct solution.

ARITHMETIC
A + B = NA plus B equals N.
A – B = NA minus B equals N. Note A > B always holds.
A * B = NA times B equals N.
COMPARISON
A > BA is greater than B.
A < BA is less than B.
PARITY & PRIMALITY
A is evenA ∈ {2, 4, 6, 8, 10}.
A is oddA ∈ {1, 3, 5, 7, 9}.
A is primeA ∈ {2, 3, 5, 7}.1 is not prime.
A is not primeA ∈ {1, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10}.
RANKING
A is the largestA is greater than all other five values.Does not mean A = 10.
A is not the largestAt least one other letter has a higher value than A.
A is the smallestA is less than all other five values.Does not mean A = 1.
A is not the smallestAt least one other letter has a lower value than A.
POSITIONAL
A is adjacent to B|A – B| = 1. They are consecutive integers.Order doesn't matter.
A is between B and Cmin(B,C) < A < max(B,C).B and C can be in any order.
A is not between B and CA < min(B,C) or A > max(B,C).B and C can be in any order.
A is closer to B than to C|A–B| < |A–C|.Does not imply A is between them.
XOR
A or B is even (not both)Exactly one of {A, B} is even; the other is odd.
A or B is prime (not both)Exactly one of {A, B} is prime; the other is not.
GLOBAL EXCLUSIONS
No two letters sum to NNo pair of values in {A,B,C,D,E,F} adds up to N.A single letter may equal N.
No two letters multiply to NNo pair of values in {A,B,C,D,E,F} multiplies to N.A single letter may equal N if no other letter is 1.
GAME MODES
  • In CASUAL mode, your rating is never affected — play freely.
  • In RATED mode, your rating updates after every puzzle, win or loss.
EXPECTED SOLVE TIME
  • Each puzzle has an expected solve time based on its rating relative to yours.
  • A puzzle rated equal to you has a baseline time. A harder puzzle (higher rating) gives you more time; an easier one (lower rating) gives you less.
  • Finishing faster than expected raises your rating. Finishing slower lowers it.
MISTAKES & PENALTIES
  • Each incorrect CHECK adds a time penalty to your effective solve time — counted against you even if you finish quickly.
  • Three mistakes triggers an automatic forfeit, which counts as a loss regardless of progress.
  • Giving up in RATED mode always counts as a loss.
RATING UNCERTAINTY (±RD)
  • Your rating starts at 1000 ± 350. The ±RD reflects how confident the system is in your rating.
  • ±RD shrinks as you play more rated puzzles, making each result have less impact.
  • During inactivity, ±RD slowly rises — the system becomes less certain, and results carry more weight again.
