SIX-FIGURE LOGIC

YOUR RATING 1000 ± 350
THE GOAL
  • Assign values to each of the six letters A through F so as to satisfy all clues.
THE RULES
  • Each letter A–F must be assigned a different integer from 1 to 10.
  • Every letter must be assigned — even those that don't appear in any clue.
  • The solution must satisfy all clues simultaneously.
  • Each puzzle has exactly one correct solution.

RATED MODE & RATING
  • Switch to Rated before generating a puzzle to affect your rating.
  • Your rating starts at 1000 and adjusts after every rated solve.
  • Solving faster than expected raises your rating; slower lowers it.
  • Each incorrect "Check Solution" adds a time penalty. Three mistakes = auto-forfeit.
  • Giving up in Rated mode counts as a loss.
  • Rating uncertainty (±RD) decreases as you play more, and slowly recovers during inactivity.

RANKING

A is not the largestAt least one other letter has a higher value than A.
A is the smallestA is less than all other five values.Does not mean A = 1.
A is not the smallestAt least one other letter has a lower value than A.
POSITIONAL
A is adjacent to B|A – B| = 1. They are consecutive integers.Order doesn't matter.
A is between B and Cmin(B,C) < A < max(B,C).B and C can be in any order.
A is not between B and CA < min(B,C) or A > max(B,C).B and C can be in any order.
A is closer to B than to C|A–B| < |A–C|.Does not imply A is between them.
XOR
A or B is even (not both)Exactly one of {A, B} is even; the other is odd.
A or B is prime (not both)Exactly one of {A, B} is prime; the other is not.
GLOBAL EXCLUSIONS
No two letters sum to NNo pair of values in {A,B,C,D,E,F} adds up to N.A single letter may equal N.
No two letters multiply to NNo pair of values in {A,B,C,D,E,F} multiplies to N.A single letter may equal N if no other letter is 1.
