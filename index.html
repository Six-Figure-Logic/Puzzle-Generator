SIX-FIGURE LOGIC

YOUR RATING: 1000 ± 350
THE GOAL
  • Each puzzle consists of a set of clues, and six unknown variables (A, B, C, D, E, F).
  • Your goal is to assign a unique number (1–10) to each of the six letters so that all clues are satisfied.
  • Each letter must be assigned a different number. No repeats.
  • Each letter must be assigned a number even if that letter does not appear in any clue.
THE ELIMINATION GRID

The grid is your scratchpad. Each row is a letter (A–F), each column is a possible value (1–10).

Left clickTap Toggle a single cell — manually eliminate (or restore) a candidate.
Right clickLong press Lock in a value — crosses out everything else in that row and column.
Ctrl + Left click Same as right click.
↺ Reset Grid Clear all eliminations and start fresh.
CHECKING YOUR ANSWER
  • Set each letter's value using the grid or the answer dropdowns.
  • Press CHECK when you think you have the solution.
  • Each clue turns green if satisfied, or red if contradicted by your inputs.
  • If all your clues turn green, you have solved the puzzle! If any clue turns red, you will receive a strike.
  • Three wrong checks (strikes) result in an automatic forfeit.
TIPS
  • Hover over any clue to see a plain-English explanation of what it means.
  • Start with the most restrictive clues — arithmetic clues (+, -, *) narrow things down fast.
  • Use the grid to cross off values you've ruled out. When a row is down to one cell, you've found that letter's value.
  • Stuck? The Tutorial walks through a full example step by step.
  • Still stuck? Press 💡HINT for help (not available in RATED games).
ARITHMETIC
A + B = NA plus B equals N.
A – B = NA minus B equals N.
A * B = NA times B equals N.
COMPARISON
A > BA is greater than B.
A < BA is less than B.
PARITY & PRIMALITY
A is evenA ∈ {2, 4, 6, 8, 10}.
A is oddA ∈ {1, 3, 5, 7, 9}.
A is primeA ∈ {2, 3, 5, 7}.1 is not prime.
A is not primeA ∈ {1, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10}.
RANKING
A is the largestA is greater than all other five values.Does not mean A = 10.
A is not the largestAt least one other letter has a higher value than A.
A is the smallestA is less than all other five values.Does not mean A = 1.
A is not the smallestAt least one other letter has a lower value than A.
POSITIONAL
A is adjacent to B|A – B| = 1. They are consecutive integers.Order doesn't matter.
A is between B and Cmin(B,C) < A < max(B,C).B and C can be in any order.
A is not between B and CA < min(B,C) or A > max(B,C).B and C can be in any order.
A is closer to B than to C|A–B| < |A–C|.Does not imply A is between them.
XOR
A or B is even (not both)Exactly one of {A, B} is even; the other is odd.
A or B is prime (not both)Exactly one of {A, B} is prime; the other is not.
GLOBAL EXCLUSIONS
No two letters sum to NNo pair of values in {A,B,C,D,E,F} adds up to N.A single letter may equal N.
No two letters multiply to NNo pair of values in {A,B,C,D,E,F} multiplies to N.A single letter may equal N if no other letter is 1.
CASUAL VS RATED
  • CASUAL — Play freely. Your rating is never affected.
  • RATED — Your rating updates after every puzzle. Available for RANDOM PUZZLES only.


DAILY PUZZLES
  • DAILY PUZZLES are always casual — no rating pressure.

HOW RATING WORKS
  • Whenever you solve a rated puzzle with 0 mistakes, your rating will increase, regardless of solve time.
  • The faster your solve time, the more points you earn (up to a limit).
  • Solving with 1 or 2 strikes (incorrect checks) may lower your rating depending on your rating vs. the puzzle's rating.
  • If you forfeit or get 3 strikes your rating decreases.

YOUR RATING (± RD)
  • Everyone starts at 1000 ± 350. The ± number is your rating uncertainty — it shrinks as you play more rated puzzles.
  • Higher uncertainty means bigger swings after each rated game, in either direction.
  • If you stop playing rated puzzles for a while, the ± number increases.
MISTAKES
  • Each wrong CHECK adds a strike and a time penalty.
  • Fewer mistakes = A better result, regardless of solve time.
  • Three mistakes triggers an automatic forfeit.
  • Forfeiting always counts as a loss.
⚠ YOUR DATA
  • Your rating, solve history, and daily puzzle progress are stored in your browser's local storage — not on any server.
  • Clearing your browser history or site data permanently deletes everything — your rating resets to 1000 ± 350 and all solve history is lost.
  • This includes Chrome's "Delete browsing data" (unless you uncheck 'cookies and other site data'), Firefox's "Clear history", Safari's "Clear history and website data", and any equivalent in other browsers.
  • Private / Incognito mode also starts fresh every session — progress made there will not be saved.
